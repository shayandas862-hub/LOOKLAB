How film 5 was made: 1920×1080 at 24 fps, cut at 120 BPM, so one beat is exactly 12 frames. The music is written in code on the same clock as the picture, which is why every cut lands on the beat. 2,208 frames rendered in 66 minutes on four ordinary processors, no graphics card. The colour check uses CIEDE2000 and matches three of the published reference pairs (Sharma, Wu and Dalal, 2005) to four decimal places. The same checks also found a flaw in film 5 itself: a light on the shoe that jumps back every 2 seconds. It's logged, not fixed yet.
