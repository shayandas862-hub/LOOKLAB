# ONE SENTENCE: the second brand film (experiment 05)

Planned 2026-10-14, made autonomously at the founder's request ("one step higher than before"). **Not part of
Look Lab:** nothing here is imported, built, tested or served by Look Lab (D-LL-056).

## The step up from ACTION

**ACTION showed what the tool could imagine. ONE SENTENCE shows the tool working:** one sentence goes in, and the
camera travels without a visible cut through the tool's own loop until a world comes out:
- **Brief:** the sentence is typed.
- **Plan:** shot cards are drawn, then unfold into data.
- **Route:** the cards fly into four engine gates.
- **Make:** the shots are built, layer by layer.
- **Check:** the checker measures the shots, catches a mistake and fixes it.
- **Cut:** the edit plays in three frame shapes.
- **World:** the camera flies into the final frame, which becomes a world.

Three things are new:
1. **One camera journey.** Every transition is a hidden cut: through the full stop, through a gate's light,
   through a screen. It reads as one unbroken move.
2. **Every number on screen is true.** Lens, frame counts, camera speed, timecode, beat count, and colour
   difference (ΔE00) are read from the film's own data and its own rendered pixels. The end card says so.
3. **A real brief.** The film makes the commercial it was asked for: a running shoe, warm, premium. You see it
   planned, built, checked, cut, and then stepped into.

## Decisions taken autonomously (the founder may overrule any of them)

| Chose | Rejected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The product's loop as the story | Another "imagine anything" montage | One step higher: it shows the system, which is what's being built and posted about |
| One continuous camera with hidden cuts | Beat cuts like ACTION | A clear step up in craft, and it tells the story as one flow |
| Real measured numbers on screen | Decorative numbers | Honesty is the brand; Look Lab measures, so the film measures too |
| Made entirely in code again, with the AI gate shown as part of the loop | Generated shots in this film | No fal key in this session; the generated-video pass is the next experiment (`../docs/NEXT-GEN-VIDEO.md`) |
| A new track in the same family: hard rock in A minor, a clean intro, a guitar solo over the checker, a gang shout of "GO!" | Reusing ACTION's track | A new film needs its own riff; the founder liked the hard-rock direction |
| The same faceless hero, now wearing the running shoe | A new character | A recurring character builds the brand |
| Title ONE SENTENCE; closing line "One sentence in. A world out." | ONE TAKE | Hidden cuts mean it isn't literally one take; the sentence is the honest hook |

## The clock

120 BPM at 24 fps, so one beat is 12 frames and one bar is 2 s. The film is 46 bars: 1:32.

## The film

| Bars | Time | Section | Music | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | 0:00–0:12 | **BRIEF** | A clean guitar arpeggio with echo, a heartbeat kick, the typing | A cursor in the dark types *a launch film for a running shoe. warm. premium.* One key per sixteenth note. The camera pushes into the full stop, which becomes a light. |
| 6–11 | 0:12–0:24 | **PLAN** | Riff and half-time drums arrive; a gang **HEY!** on the hits | Four storyboard cards hang in space, sketched live by a glowing pencil: dune at dawn, stride, product, city at night. Each unfolds into its shot package: lens, frames, light, a wireframe of the set. |
| 12–13 | 0:24–0:28 | **ROUTE** | Toms, a snare roll, silence, **"GO!"** | The cards fly into four gates: CODE, 3D, AI, CAMERA. The camera follows one card into a gate's light. |
| 14–21 | 0:28–0:44 | **MAKE** | The drop: the full band | Four sets in a row, each built on the beat as a scan line sweeps across it (wireframe to clay to lit to final), then filmed by its own shot camera. The camera whips from set to set. |
| 22–27 | 0:44–0:56 | **CHECK** | Half-time; a guitar solo | A gallery of the four shots with live measurements. Shot 1's shoe is off-brand: **ΔE00 measured ✗**. It's fixed and measured again: **✓**. Every number comes from the pixels. |
| 28–33 | 0:56–1:08 | **CUT** | Driving | The shots fly onto a timeline beside the score's waveform; the playhead runs; the cut plays at once in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1. |

| 36–43 | 1:12–1:28 | **WORLD** | Everything, biggest; the lead harmonised | Through the frame into the dune world itself: the hero runs past in the shoe as the sun comes up, and the camera orbits and cranes. **ONE SENTENCE.** |
| 44–45 | 1:28–1:32 | **END** | The final hit and its ring-out | *One sentence in. A world out.* · *every number on screen was measured* · *built in public* |
