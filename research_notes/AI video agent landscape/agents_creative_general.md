## 1. Who sells or demos an end-to-end agent for this kind of video today?

### Takeaway
About 35 startup or mid-size products now sell something they call a video "agent" or a one-prompt video maker. They fall into five groups. (a) Agent studios for professionals: invideo Agent Two, Krea Agent, Flova, Hedra Agent, FLORA's FAUNA, Glif, TapNow, Lovart. (b) One-prompt explainer and social generators: Medeo, Mootion, Atlabs, Zebracat, Revid, Fliki, Pictory, Steve.ai, Vyond Go, Visla, Vadoo, Hypernatural, Agent Opus, Wondercraft. (c) Story and narrative tools: OpenArt One-Click Story and Director, Showrunner, Story.com, MovieFlo, LTX Studio, Katalist, Morphic. (d) Music-video tools: Freebeat, Neural Frames, Atlabs MV, Vidu Agent. (e) Node or canvas tools with no end-to-end agent: Weavy/Figma Weave, Freepik Spaces, Kaiber Superstudio. Several of these have pivoted, been acquired or changed name. Weavy went to Figma (2025-10-30). Manus's own pages say it is "now part of Meta" (unverified). Showrunner moved to showrunnerstudio.com. Mootion has become paid-only.

### Inferences
- "Agent" is now a marketing word. In groups (b) and (c) it mostly means an LLM writes a script and assembles stock or generated clips. In group (a) it means the system plans, picks a model for each shot and keeps memory. Only Krea documents a review-and-retry loop (section 2).
- The client's exact pitch already has a direct, funded competitor. invideo Agent Two names "filmmakers, marketing teams, Meta ads teams, and agencies" as its users (search summary of app listing, section 2). So does Flova, which is aimed at professional and long-form production.

## 2. For each product: what it really does end to end, output, manual work, price, users, clarifying questions, self-checks, preference learning, and the stage of each claim

### Takeaway
No product documents the whole chain, from one brief through script, storyboard, shots, voice, music, SFX, edit, colour, titles, three aspect ratios and long and short cuts, together with self-judgement and preference learning. invideo Agent Two documents the most production stages, plus project memory and continuity flagging. Krea Agent is the only one that documents checking its own output and retrying, and remembering preferences. Atlabs, Medeo and Mootion document the most finishing features (voice, music, subtitles, aspect ratios, Premiere export) for explainer and social video. **Colour grading is documented by no one.** Most commercial-use terms were not stated on pricing pages, so treat them as unclear.

## 5. Which 3–5 come closest to all four promises, and what evidence shows how good the output really is?

### Takeaway
The closest five are invideo Agent Two (11), Krea Agent (10), Atlabs (9), Flova (9), and a tie at 8 between OpenArt Director and Medeo. invideo is the closest match to the client's "Cursor for generative video" pitch. It has project memory, role agents, guided workflows, choice among 200+ models, continuity flagging and reframing. Its own numbers, though, show that a human still selects: about 3 generations per usable shot, and 25% of clips used. The independent evidence is thin and mostly negative on credit burn and literal accuracy. No product shows strong independent proof that people like the output without heavy human selection.

### Cited Findings
- **invideo Agent Two, own data.** "41 of 164 generated clips made the final cut", which is 25%. The team "averaged 3 generations per usable shot". The agent "generates without asking" by default, and real spend can be "3-4x higher" than a one-generation-per-shot budget — [invideo FAQ [P], updated 2026-09-09](https://invideo.io/faq/why-does-invideo-agent-one-run-out-of-credits/).

### Inferences
- **Honest answer for the client.** The idea "already exists" in parts, sold by funded companies:
  - invideo Agent Two covers plan, choose an engine, generate, and check continuity, for filmmakers and agencies.
  - Krea covers checking and retrying, and remembering preferences.
  - Atlabs, Medeo and Mootion cover one prompt to a finished short with sound, in several sizes.

  No one has put all four promises together with independent proof of quality. The weakest links across the market are:
  - automatic taste and on-brief judgement with retry;
  - learning one client's preferences over time;
  - colour grading;
  - automatic long and short cut-downs;
  - Indian-language support, which is unverified.
- invideo's 25% clip-use rate is the best available benchmark for what "self-checking" would need to beat. An agent that filtered out the other ~75% of takes itself, before a human looked, would answer promise 4 directly. It would also cut the 3–4x credit overrun.
- For a production company, credit economics matter as much as quality. Every leading product bills per generation, not per approved shot. The evidence is invideo's FAQ, Medeo's credit complaints and Genspark's billing complaints.

### Gaps
- No independent, blind quality comparison of these agents was found. User evidence is mostly star ratings and complaint snippets read through search summaries.
- Reddit threads (r/aivideo, r/VideoEditing, r/SaaS) could not be read directly. They appear only second-hand.
- India- and UK-specific pricing (INR or GBP) was not shown on any pricing page read. The search budget ran out before regional and Indian-language checks.
