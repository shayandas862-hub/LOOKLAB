# 06 · DAS PRODUCTION: a 3D studio ident, made entirely in code

A 14-second opening logo for Das Production, the founder's film production company, in the tradition of a studio
ident before a feature. It's built from the company's own flat logo (a red leaf with a hand-drawn D, three small
leaves in green, amber and cyan, and the words "as production"), rebuilt in 3D and animated. Nothing is sampled
or generated by a model: picture and sound are code.

**Not part of Look Lab.** Nothing here is imported, built, tested or served by Look Lab (D-LL-056).

## What happens

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Four lacquered petals swirl through a dark, hazy space, tumbling, the red one sweeping past the lens | A low D drone and moving air; a whoosh each time a petal passes the lens, panned to where it is on screen |
| 0:05–0:07 | They fall out of the swirl and lock into the logo one by one: green, amber, cyan | A swoosh and a deep thud for each; a riser building under them |
| 0:07.2 | The red petal locks last: lens streak, sparks, a shock ring, light behind it | The big hit: a sub drop, a crash, and a brass-like D major chord |
| 0:07.5–0:08.8 | The D is drawn on the red petal in molten gold by a glowing pen tip, then cools | A shimmer that climbs in pitch with the pen |
| 0:08.8–0:10.4 | "as", then "production", stand up letter by letter | A bell per letter, climbing the D major pentatonic |
| 0:10.5–0:11.6 | A glint sweeps across the whole logo | A bright "shing" from left to right |
| 0:11.6–0:14 | Hold on the hero angle with a slow push in; fade | The chord's tail; fade |

## How close it is to the logo

- **Shapes and layout:** measured from the logo image in pixels and placed at the same proportions. Each petal is
  a leaf with one pair of opposite corners sharp and the other pair rounded, alternating as in the original.
- **The D:** traced by hand along the marker stroke as two strokes (stem, then bowl), with the knobs at the stem's
  ends.
- **Colours:** sampled from the logo (median of a patch inside each shape) and then checked in the rendered hero
  frame. Lights were set by measurement, not by eye: the first renders came out at about half the logo's
  lightness. The final numbers are under "Measured" below.
- **The lettering:** the logo's words look like Georgia Bold, which is a Microsoft font and isn't used here.
  [Gelasio](https://github.com/SorkinType/Gelasio) Bold, made as Georgia's open-licence match, stands in. If the
  original font file and its licence are available, it's a one-line swap in `src/logo.js`.

- **Render:** 336 frames in 1,674 s (28 min) with 4 browsers; finish about 2 min.
- **Sound:** -14.4 LUFS, peaks -1.3 dBFS. Checked as a spectrogram, waveform and section levels; not by ear.
- **Motion:** the camera tops out at 2.1 m/s and turns at most 0.11 rad/s.

## Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `src/timeline.js` | Brand colours, the measured layout, the D's strokes, the words, every cue |
| `src/logo.js` | The 3D logo: extruded leaf petals with colour gradients, the D as gold tubes, letters from the font's outlines |
| `src/motion.js` | Where each petal and the camera are at any time (the score reads it too) |
| `src/ident.js` | The ident as one function of time: lights, the swirl, the locks, the drawing, the letters, the glint |
| `src/fx.js` | Dust, sparks, shock rings, haze, the glow behind the logo and the god rays |
| `src/engine.js` | Renderer, motion blur, bloom, tone mapping and the grade (with a lens streak) |
| `audio/score.mjs` | The sound, from the same clock and motion |

## Run it

You need Node 22+, ffmpeg and Google Chrome (`brew install node ffmpeg` on a Mac).

```sh
cd "incubator/video-harness-experiments/06-das-ident"
npm install
node audio/score.mjs                      # → out/score.wav (about 3 s)
node render.mjs stills 3 7.4 12.5         # check single frames → out/stills/
node render.mjs video --workers 4         # → out/silent.mp4
node render.mjs stills 12.5 && bash finish.sh   # master, 1080p, 720p, hero still, contact sheet, loudness
node verify.mjs                           # the motion checker
```

### Changing one moment without rendering everything again

- **Preview first:** `PREVIEW=1 node render.mjs clip 6.5 9.5` renders just that stretch without motion blur, about
  three times faster, with the sound under it.
- **Then re-render only what changed:** the full render is made of 2-second pieces, `out/chunk_000.mp4` to
  `chunk_006.mp4`, each with a `.done` marker. Delete only the pieces whose seconds the change touches, and run
  `node render.mjs video` again; the other pieces are reused as they are.
- **If the old pieces are gone,** cut them from the previous `out/silent.mp4`, losslessly (every piece starts on a
  keyframe): `ffmpeg -ss 10 -i silent.mp4 -frames:v 48 -c copy chunk_005.mp4 && touch chunk_005.mp4.done`.
- **Whole-ident changes** (lighting, colour, the camera) touch every frame, so they still need the full render.
